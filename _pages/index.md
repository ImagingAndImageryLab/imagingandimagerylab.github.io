The Imaging & Imagery Lab studies how the brain builds the world we call reality — how
imagination and perception meet, and what that tells us about conscious experience.

The lab is based in the Department of Psychology and Behavioral Sciences at Zhejiang
University. We combine brain imaging with **EEG, TMS and psychophysical paradigms** to ask
what imagination is made of, how it is generated, and how it becomes available to awareness.

We welcome anyone who is curious about these questions, whether or not you already have
research experience — training and support are part of what the lab is for.
